# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in PayTrack a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behaviour:
- Welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect for differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Constructive, specific feedback
- Focusing on the code and the project — not the person

Unacceptable behaviour:
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks
- Harassment — public or private
- Publishing others' private information

## Enforcement

Report violations to **conduct@paytrack.dev**. All complaints will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, or ban contributors who violate this code.
